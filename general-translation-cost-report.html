Simple assumptions

123 meetings is Civic Cycle's observed meeting volume over the last 365 days; 96 are currently summarized. AI average comes from 13,035 recorded calls across 2,506 meetings. Meeting-translation size is estimated from 1,745 stored summaries at about four characters per token. Cache hits cost no additional GT Runtime translation, so repeat page views are not charged again.

Not included

Hosting, Supabase plan fees, taxes, human language review, engineering maintenance, retries, and optional GT Platform Context. Actual General Translation allowance and invoice credits remain account-specific.
